    How the prototype works

    Finds where a group pays two prices for one item

    An AI agent for procurement. It checks a buyer's tip against every order, proves the gap like for like and proposes how to close it. A person decides; nothing is sent.

    The company is invented, the arithmetic is real

    GlobalCorp SE and its data are fictional. The replay runs the agent's written steps through its live tools, so the engine recomputes every figure. Start agent offers a live run with Claude on your own key.

    Where everything comes from
    FictionalGlobalCorp SE: 4 plants, 132 suppliers, 1,540 orders, and the agent's words in the replay
    RealThe engine's arithmetic, the ECB's exchange rates and, with your key, Claude itself
    In productionOrders from SAP S/4HANA or Ariba, the engine on Microsoft Fabric, the agent on Azure OpenAI, approvals in Teams

    Show it in five minutes

    It plays by itself and stops where you act; Space pauses while you talk.

    1. The tip: a buyer's hunch. Point at the four figures. “The gap's worth and width, your time to decide, the agent's cost.” 1 min
    2. Like for like. “Birmingham's £6.20 per 100 looks cheap. In euros, delivered, it's €7.56.” 1 min
    3. The gap. “Lyon pays €9.80, the contract €6.90: €14,146 a year no reason explains.” 1 min
    4. Your decision: approve the recommended option. “Savings of €12,501 a year, the first after 30 days.” 1 min
    5. The drafts: approve both. “The agent writes them. Nothing leaves without a person's approval.” 1 min
